---
name: Payroll onboarding scope
description: New employees and the demonstration account need the payroll form; existing staff were handled manually.
---

The payroll information form is a checklist step for newly created employee accounts, after the existing personal-rules consent gate. Do not add it retroactively to existing real employees. The demonstration employee account is an explicit exception.

**Why:** The user said existing employees had already received the link manually, while new employees should no longer need manual delivery.

**How to apply:** Assign the step at account creation, not based on first login or whether other onboarding tasks are incomplete. Preserve all historical personal-rules acceptances and existing completed checklist steps.

Opening an external payroll form does not prove it was submitted.

**Why:** Payroll depends on actual information reaching the form owner, and the app has no verified Google Forms submission integration.

**How to apply:** Keep opening the form separate from the employee's explicit confirmation of submission. Never describe that confirmation as automatically verified receipt of payroll details.
